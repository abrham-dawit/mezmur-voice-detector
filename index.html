How the real version would work

  1. Fingerprint the audio. Turn the clip into a compact audio fingerprint (the way Shazam does) or a feature embedding with a small neural net.
  2. Match against a labeled library. Compare the fingerprint to a database of known Tigrinya/Amharic songs and mezmur, each labeled with title, singer, and where it's usually sung.
  3. Return the best match with a confidence. If nothing clears the confidence bar, the app should say "not sure" instead of guessing — especially for mezmur, where different churches sing the same hymn differently.

What a real model needs (that doesn't exist yet)

  • A labeled dataset: hundreds of recordings of Tigrinya/Amharic songs and mezmur, each tagged with the correct title — recording rights and church/community permission matter here.
  • Variation coverage: the same mezmur sung by different choirs, at different speeds, in living rooms and churches with reverb and crowd noise.
  • Either an audio-fingerprinting pipeline (good for exact recordings) or a trained classifier (better for live singing of the same hymn) — probably both.
  • People from the community checking the matches. A wrong title on a religious song isn't a small error.
